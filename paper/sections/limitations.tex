\section{Limitations}
Training is dominated by substituted Wikipedia examples and includes AI-authored text, whereas development is natural parliamentary text. Domain, construction and acronym composition differ together. Document overlap and shared morphological families (Section~\ref{sec:separation}) limit separation claims; pretraining exposure is also unknown.

Development has only 62 items across 11 unevenly represented types. Five singleton types contribute five elevenths of macro accuracy, while the largest type contributes 15 of 62 items to micro accuracy. A single stored gold label per type does not demonstrate semantic uniformity: known annotation defects prevent reliable inference about the true sense distribution. The sample and labels therefore cannot establish stable performance across types or robust contextual sense discrimination.

Inspection exposed concrete development-label and inventory defects. One \heb{מט״ח} sentence explicitly denotes \mbox{\heb{המרכז לטכנולוגיה חינוכית}} (the educational technology center) but is labeled \mbox{\heb{מטבע חוץ}} (foreign currency). All nine \heb{חמ״ס} inventories omit \mbox{\heb{חומרים מסוכנים}} (hazardous materials), including contexts that spell it out; matching the stored label can therefore reward an unsuitable expansion. These observations can both depress and inflate label-based accuracy. They are findings about the inspected dev items, not evidence of the same defects in test.

Named uses require a separate distinction. The recorded decision accepts \mbox{\heb{רבי משה בן מימון}} for \heb{רמב״ם} even in hospital and street contexts. A hospital description may identify a referent without meeting that expansion convention. Disagreement on these items is therefore not automatically a labeling error, and a rule should not be changed retrospectively to favor a system.

Candidate access is coupled to response format, and the encoder and LLMs differ in supervision and architecture. Strict parsing can reject a clear intended selection, while an inventory may omit a valid expansion. These comparisons do not isolate the effect of candidate information or model size. A per-form training-majority baseline is undefined for exact forms absent from training; a relevant alternative still needs to be specified.
