\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\paragraph{Data and labels.} Natural and authored text have different construction and review histories. At least two development \heb{מט״ח} items about the educational technology center are labeled foreign currency. Development also accepts \heb{רבי משה בן מימון} for \heb{רמב״ם} in hospital and street contexts. Three test references are demonstrably wrong: \texttt{kn4-0299} and \texttt{nat-0041} concern overseas volunteers, not the political party recorded for \heb{מח״ל}; \texttt{kn4-0207} uses \heb{ס״ב} for page 62, not ``top secret.'' Review status did not prevent these errors. Historical labels remain frozen, with a separate sensitivity check in Results. Suspected \heb{עכו״ם} variants remain unresolved; alternate labels illustrate ranking fragility, not an established correction.

\paragraph{Measurement and comparison.} Scoring and selective review preclude a common semantic ranking; DictaLM also has a template mismatch. Models differ in supervision, architecture, size and inference budgets. No latency or memory advantage was measured, and one run does not estimate variability. Incomplete historical training and similarity-run records constrain reproducibility and separation claims. Broader conclusions require additional configurations and independently validated, common semantic evaluation.
