\subsection{Generation versus selection}
\label{sec:res-formats}
Candidate selection beats free generation for both LLMs. Accuracy rises from XX\% to 59.8\% for Qwen and from XX\% to 93.4\% for Gemini. % TODO: one sentence on why, from the error analysis, e.g. where generated answers go wrong
Because selection is constrained to the inventory, this gain also depends on the inventory containing the right answer, which holds for every test item (the oracle is 100\%).

\subsection{Fine-tuning and the encoder}
\label{sec:res-encoder}
Fine-tuning raises the encoder from 64.3\% to 71.7\%, a gain of 7.4 points from fewer than 3,000 training items. The fine-tuned encoder beats Qwen's selection (59.8\%) and Gemini's generation (XX\%). It trails Gemini's selection (93.4\%) by 21.7 points. So a model about 35 times smaller than Qwen can beat Qwen at selection, but it does not match the best system. We ran each system once, so we cannot say how stable the 7.4-point gain is.
