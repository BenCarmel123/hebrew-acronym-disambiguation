\section{Results and Discussion}
\label{sec:results}
The saved development comparison contains 62 completed DictaBERT selections and 62 responses for each Qwen task. Gemini selection contains 13 responses, 20 service-error items and 29 unattempted items; generation contains two service-error items and 60 unattempted items. These are execution counts, not final-test findings. Qwen generation remains semantically unjudged, and the partial Gemini subset cannot support a complete-system accuracy comparison. The development scores and item-level diagnostics remain in the companion results package.

\paragraph{Final results pending.}
The final comparison requires the test population, run identities, coverage and judgments to be confirmed. Historical scores and software fixtures are not substitutes for those results.

No conclusion about the preferred formulation or final system ranking is drawn from the development diagnostics.
